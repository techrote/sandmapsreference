# GOLDMAPS owner decision guide

Every map and every variant begins **Pending**. The 25-map Gold view is the
frozen recommendation, not a collection of accepted maps. Your decisions remain
in your isolated local evaluation workspace. Exporting a decision does not
change GitHub, a source map, an experiment verdict or the shared corpus.

## Decide one identity at a time

Confirm the visible title, variant, source PR/commit, Setup SHA-256 and runtime
identity. Open the map, inspect its material geometry, fly the routes that matter
to you, and save edited evaluation copies under new names. A saved copy is your
new variant; the bundled source stays intact.

Use **confirmed**, **declined** or **needs review** for a Gold nominee. Use the
classification field only when you intend to make an explicit classification:

| Choice | Recovered programme meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Gold | An original or otherwise explicitly nominated architectural exemplar that you choose to retain. The source identity and its limitations remain attached. |
| Platinum | An intervention you judge to be a demonstrated improvement while retaining source identity, useful topology, protected space and technical validity. A candidate or positive research result alone is insufficient. |
| Pyrite | An intervention you judge to remain substandard or to regress. Keep the derivative and the reason; this does not condemn or replace its original. |
| No-change | A useful decision to retain an already strong source. The three controls deliberately preserve their material planes. |
| Inconclusive / needs review | The available evidence or your inspection does not settle the intended improvement. |
| Other | Your own explicitly named category. It is not silently mapped to Gold, Platinum or Pyrite. |

The frozen [experiment ledger](https://github.com/techrote/sandmapsreference/blob/5982fa553bc6356742cfea0911c848c372dcdb97/docs/corpus/astra-trial-001/README.md)
used provisional Platinum Candidate, Pyrite Candidate, No-change and Inconclusive
as recommendations. Its [preregistered criteria](https://github.com/techrote/sandmapsreference/blob/5982fa553bc6356742cfea0911c848c372dcdb97/docs/corpus/astra-trial-001/experiment-manifest.v1.json)
separate technical validity, routes, structural credibility, functional
relationships, identity, negative space, corpus value and native evidence.
The guide above summarizes that usage; the neutral decision and notes fields
remain available whenever you prefer to reserve classification.

## Record useful evidence

Keep the decision, preferred variant, presentation notes and playability notes
separate. A useful note names the route/site, what you observed, the saved-copy
label and any screenshot. Your local decision export should include the date,
source and runtime identities, original Setup digest and your explicit choices.

Static geometry checks, hosted native execution and hosted graphical evidence
are different scopes. Your physical Windows/NVIDIA presentation, subjective
full-route playfeel and final classifications remain owner observations.
