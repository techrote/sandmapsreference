# GOLD-ASTRA owner comparison guide

These eight comparisons preserve the completed experiment. Open the **Astra
comparisons** view, select an identity and choose **Compare variants**. The
browser provides ordinary Workbench opens for qualified variants and named
archived site images with coordinate bounds. You can record a new local
preference without changing the frozen research result.

Every classification begins pending. A historical preference, current native
qualification and your own artistic or gameplay judgement are separate facts.

B2 5,195; B3 2,376; D1 15,807; D2 7,692. The total is 34,353. The C1–C3 material
planes are byte-identical. **Changed-cell volume is not an improvement score.**

The architectural export retains five failed interventions as negative evidence.

hash-only. Failed B1 attempt 1 and B3 attempt 1 geometry remains unavailable.
