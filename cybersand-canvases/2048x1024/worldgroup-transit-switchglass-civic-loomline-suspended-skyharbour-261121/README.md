## Spatial thesis
A ceiling-hung mechanical sky-harbour instead of another ground settlement. The suspended diaphragm creates physically entered upper/lower districts. Two wide skylight portals and an auxiliary passenger relief descent allow distinct routes, with nonradial dispatch loom, asymmetrical east freight toll, open underdeck yard and four excavated workshops. The broken transfer spar and unfilled central void are deliberate edit/set-piece affordances.
