## Structural thesis and exemplar value

A deep dry geological chamber is serviced from its basalt ceiling rather than its floor. Three differently occupied roof-hung colonies (inhabited west clinic, seismographic middle relay, sterile eastern freight/quarantine) frame two large **unequal counter-mass hoists**. Geothermal pump service and lower seismic record equipment are floor-rooted. The surrounding negative space is deliberately traversable, with upper cluster visits, extended underflight, and a separate bottom return. The machine footprint and scaffold geometry provide modular append zones without furnishing every cavity.

This differs from the prior Frontier corpus's nested underground colonies, reflector ring, two wind sails and descending grounded terraces. Eight controlled diversity axes are documented in [exemplar.json](exemplar.json). Scriptable but **unscripted** edit sites include the roof suspension failure, inhabited rescue arrival, central ballast activation and seismic archive escape. Source machinery remains art-authored material cells, not painted screenshot objects.
