A wide, internally ventilated vacuum-arc steelworks built across an excavated pressure-return tunnel. **West ore barge and charge skip** (spawn 89,249) feeds a **giant upright magnetic confinement halo** (centre near 707,500) enclosing a completely sealed arc crucible, which serves an **overhead dry guided ladle/tundish bay** near x1050; the **unequal split-strand casting and roller beds** x1240–1550 feed a **dual insulated water-quench cell** x1560–1760, before **east freight dispatch** (goal 1953,249). Operator consoles, valves, guide posts, inspection ports and four recognisably different belowdeck service plants give each district a distinct physical purpose.

The main upper flyway uses the open industrial roof plenum; **two 105-cell-wide vertical escape shafts** (x349–453 and x1800–1904) connect to an independently navigable 154-cell-high lower recovery passage. The real vertical load spine from the roof to the casting-deck floor, the transverse underground pressure-lock and the distinct lower blower, intercooler, filter bank and pump return explain load paths and make the lower bypass functional rather than a decorative scenery strip. No false platform tops, isometric contours, scenery-only actors, unsupported foundations or uncontrolled fluids.
