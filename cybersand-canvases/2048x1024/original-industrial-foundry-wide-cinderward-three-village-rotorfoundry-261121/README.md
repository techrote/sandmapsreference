# Cinderward Three-Village Rotoryard

New original, not reference-led, 2048×1024 strictly perpendicular side-elevation material-cell world. Three differentiated industrial townships occupy the lower plane. The west ore village feeds sealed refractory furnaces and a travelling ladle; upper casters feed a huge central floor-rooted radial rotor, sealed east quench, and export rail. Deliberately vacant district seams are SWARM/GENTEK and manual editing opportunities, not missing scenery. Four potential set pieces are unscripted. Exact 4-neighbour whole-hull Midge proofs and a physical obstruction are stored in receipt.json. `exemplar.json` records the design-corpus classifications.
