## Identity and new spatial idea

This world is an interior-dominant, crooked survey installation cut through a single continuous geological body. A diagonal, multi-strata primary route enters an inhabited rover intake, climbs four **differently proportioned embedded scientific calibration galleries**, passes signal-comparator and cartography equipment, and reaches the quarantined eastern exit. An independent **long lower serrated emergency adit** branches from the western entrance, remains separated through most of the mountain, and rejoins by an eastern rescue ascent. Side branches open to a spare geode, an acoustic expansion bay, and a single small exposed observation slit.

**Design-language only, never geometry:** Sable Quoin Gravitic Core (calibration/gravimetric instruments, diagnostic keys, severe basalt engineering) and Threnody Scar Foldwing Seedlift (xenobiological custody, quarantine and sealed science freight). We did not import their cells, pixels, rectangles, coordinates, route graphs, buildings, or full district plans. Quoin Veil Sibling 01 (Suspended Shear Basin) was independently inspected and deliberately *not* repeated: no opposed scarp laboratories, three hanging midair rafts, V basin or cable harp.

**Workface districts:** west protected rover/seismic ingress; Gallery A embedded bedded resonator and spare northern geode; Gallery B low core sampling plus entry interlocks; Gallery C asymmetric dual-phase comparator and unfurnished acoustics annex; Gallery D stepped high cartography with exterior observation cut; east quarantine/dispatch; and several unequal equipment platforms along the emergency adit.
