## Structural design thesis and diversity axes

This is **three offset terrain-embedded terraces**, not another open low linear quay, hanging arkship, twin-cistern ballast plant, tipper or V-cut salvage basin. The high west inspection switchback, the offset central gravity sorter, and the eastern highland export shelf have staggered altitude, rooted basalt, load-bearing transfer masts, diagonal grade exchanges and carved interior inspection chambers. An upper atmospheric bypass, main freight route, lower rock-cut facilities and a deep utility return give separately useful route strata.

The three intentionally underrepresented corpus dimensions are **nested/terraced landform topology**, **high/low/diagonal switchback altitude grammar**, and **massive carved geology against intentionally open transfer gaps**. This is a starting point for future *manual human editing*, SWARM/GENTEK exemplar extraction and scripted-setpiece staging, not an exhaustively furnished finished level. Some quiet bays, reserved roof terraces and a central open countergrade are deliberate.
