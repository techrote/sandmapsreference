## Five functional districts

1. **Inbound rake and west three-way fork** - entry collector, route-grade switch, material actuator contacts and a physical lower turnout workshop.
2. **West selector and tally** - elevated but short open signal workface, three-track decision, grounded signal pylons and alternate fault drop.
3. **Central comparator/interlocking** - mechanically legible inspection and classification contacts with an upper signal gallery and a lower comparator service pit.
4. **Divergent dispatch** - differently pitched return/outbound branches, short catenary controls, accessible slip-coupling service chamber.
5. **East recovery/turnback** - failure-return track, low emergency traction plant, east turn and west-bound alternative route.

All are true orthographic material-cell structures with intentionally open flight voids, founded loads and real enterable service chambers. Machines are static mechanical architecture; no simulated movement or interactive switch behavior is claimed. Zinc yellow indicates impending fork choices; ivory marks recovery and survival routes.
