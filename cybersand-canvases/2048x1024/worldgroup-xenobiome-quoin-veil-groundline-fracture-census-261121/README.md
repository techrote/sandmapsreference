## Sibling role and distinct spatial thesis

A multi-settlement geological census and habitation district, distributed across **three grounded, low-rise scientific communities** rather than a single giant apparatus. The west quarter includes resident bunks, a specimen sorter and survey transponder; the inhabited infield has an open civic census hall, assay court, shallow fracture gauges and a vacant append seam; the eastern quarter has biological quarantine, seed custody, long cargo handling and a partly collapsed standalone store. The upper skyline remains broad, with no canyon-scaled V cut or cliff-embedded steep survey spine.

Three offset shallow faults interrupt a continuously inhabited **horizontal groundline**. A roofline flyover and two long, deliberately separate buried service lanes with end lifts and transverse drops create actual alternate circulation and stageable rescue volumes. Four differentiated districts/workfaces are west habitation, census/registration commons, assay/fault gauging, and east sterile dispatch; lower maintenance supplies additional workfaces. Empty infield bays and the damaged outbuilding remain intentionally editable.

**Family language, not geometry:** the actual current Sable Quoin and Threnody Scar anchor revisions supply only archaeological-science instrument vocabulary, xenoseed handling and quarantined research culture. No pixels, coordinates, rectangle streams, routes, district outlines, roof plans or layouts have been imported. Unlike Quoin Veil Sibling 01 [Suspended Shear Basin](https://github.com/techrote/sandmapsreference/pull/108), this is neither a facing-cliff V basin nor a suspended raft network. Unlike Sibling 02 [Oblique Fault Cartography](https://github.com/techrote/sandmapsreference/pull/114), this is not an interior-dominant steep geological ascent.
