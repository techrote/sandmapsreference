## Place and working principle

Cinderwake is an inland ocean-freight transshipment complex cut into a basalt shelf. A drydock receives vessels on cradles; an immense cantilever crane transfers hull modules to a blue trussed export railway. Loaded containers cross a two-jaw counterweighted lift into an asymmetrical, rising classifier bank. A sawtooth customs terminal sends them to the eastern rail lands, while a service railway below the rock provides a return route independent of the exposed gantry. Rust-dark geological bedrock, cyan painted rails, alloy load paths, glass signal boxes and low amber indicators distinguish function instead of simply wallpapering the map.

All foreground construction is **orthographic perpendicular side elevation**. No visible roof or platform top plane, perspective or isometric gameplay cells. The central crane and freight-lift profiles are deliberately taller than typical doors; traversable shafts and rooms are considerably smaller and are calibrated against the **4×3 authored Midge hull**, with clearance audited at the larger 19×15, 23×19 and 31×27 centre footprints.

## District graph and actual-world design

1. **West Drydock & Keel** (x0–510): side-walled excavated ship basin, working cradle, deep open air, starboard load pier, two keel approaches and a wall-braced cantilever crane. Primary sites: West quay, West arrival lock, Drydock keel, West crane bridge.
2. **Blue Gantry & Customs Rail** (x510–925): lower rail train, truss carrying load into ground, containers of unequal lengths, glazed staffed customs gallery and sky crane. Functional sites: Rail departure, Customs gallery, West transfer throat.
3. **Counterweighted Inland Freight Lift** (x925–1250): the distinct twin tapered jaw towers, crowned transfer loop, separate upper/middle/undercroft access, grounded outriggers and cable-fed rails. Functional sites: Lift crown, Lift middle, Lift undercroft.
4. **Classification Hump & Signal Control** (x1250–1720): rising cargo-sorting relief, differently sized cargo troughs, signal hall, anchored overhead crane and long diagonal structural load branches. Functional sites: East classifier, Signal hall, East rail siding.
5. **East Sawtooth Terminal** (x1720–2048): asymmetric roof rhythm, partially enclosed wagon works, elevated assay station and exit freight tunnel; structure reaches world-side geology. Functional sites: Terminal arrival, Terminal upper assay, East exit tunnel.
6. **Excavated Subterranean Return** (x100–1950, y670–880): five walled, engineered railway service chambers with supporting ceilings/grounds, lit inset service niches, cross-region passages and five vertical Midge-flight transfer shafts. Functional sites: West subway, Lift undercroft, East subway and East exit tunnel.

**Horizontal primary navigation** traverses the sky and upper works from west-to-east, with descents into rail/commercial rooms. **Alternative return** runs through the lower dry service artery, with vertical transfers near x464, 861, 1084, 1418 and 1836. These are open flying/maintenance shafts, not asserted climbable ladders. A physical 31×27-obstruction test temporarily inserts steel into the high cross-harbour route and checks an actual full-footprint cardinal detour; the obstruction is **not** saved into the final world. Structural supports were revised after diagnosis: the entire occupied plane is now attached through four-neighbour solid cells to at least one world boundary (not a native stability experiment).

## Build and verify

Use Node 22 on a fresh checkout of this branch:

```bash
D="cybersand-canvases/2048x1024/original-industrial-transit-wide-cinderwake-dual-harbour-interchange-261119"
node "$D/build.cjs" --verify
node "$D/verify-independent.cjs"
git diff --exit-code -- "$D"
```

The builder uses fixed seed **20261119**, creates the entire material-ID array, encodes nonoverlapping material rectangles and emits the Setup-v2 file, physical receipt, full-grid indexed material PNG and five cropped true 1:1 PNGs. The verifier **does not run/import the builder**; it independently reads the serialized JSON, rebuilds every cell, validates exact destinations and full hull clearance, checks alternate passage and all emitted PNG bytes, and records grounded-vs-detached solid counts. GitHub Actions publishes its own generated artifacts only on the exclusive lane branch, never on `main`, and executes both checks.
