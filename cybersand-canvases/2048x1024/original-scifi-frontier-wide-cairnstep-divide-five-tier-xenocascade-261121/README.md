## Distinctive structural thesis
The geological rock staircase descends across multiple uneven inhabited terraces to a higher eastern relief approach. Six individual physical districts host rover rescue/medical, biospecimen assay, ore/xenoparticle sorting, biological filtering, contamination quarantine and evacuation freight. Two-pier grounded platforms follow the changing natural surface; asymmetrically rooted threshold gates are not painted scenery. Broad interdistrict flight gaps and usable but partly unfinished lower verandas are intentional authoring affordances.

Unlike a central ring or excavated nested-cave system, the principal spatial form is a dry multistage terrain cascade. Routes use primary terrace approaches, overflight above all the gantries and underdeck/low-landscape open passages. There are 24 literal tested destinations, including spawn `West airlock [186,508]`; a real hypothetical solid obstruction over High mixing switch leaves 23 other sites reachable at 31×27.
