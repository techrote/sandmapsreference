## Spatial thesis / districts

Unlike previous caldera/cantilever-vessel/underground-tower/mesa work, this level is a genuinely split *geological fault*: two offset root-connected fractured cliffs across a large negative-space chasm, with a half-broken mid-level cantilever pair and a lower service strut. A pair of differently shaped bored interior plants are real open material cells. Equipment, adjacent rooms and trusses derive only from exact native material IDs, never from visual overlays. Distinct districts: (1) west launch and switchhouse; (2) west shear-bluff control rooms; (3) central fault tensile bridge; (4) east conductor and bellows caverns; (5) east braking catcher / inversion yard. Additional deep western/eastern inspection pockets and shoulder seams are deliberately unfilled.

## Midge routes / intentional switchback

**Primary:** west launch → named high west/east skyline relay sites → eastern catcher → eastern rise to crest → western side of east cliff → drop into central fault → westbound bore/underworks → west finish. The *eastern rise before the low westward return is intentional*: a load-bearing catenary would block a fictitious direct east-bore crossing. The upper-midlevel alternative branches at west cusp, enters the broken bridge fault gap at `Midlevel west approach`, `Midlevel fault breach`, `Midlevel east approach`, and rejoins the eastern crest. A real obstruction `[987,185,99,112]` covers `High fault witness` while the alternative, finish and remaining exact sites survive. Topology verified by conservative 19×15, 23×19, 31×27 free-centre 4-neighbour eroded-grid reachability and independent **240-pixel locally bounded 31×27 cardinal path legs**, not native flight dynamics.

**Setpiece and manual edit potential** (no events scripted): fracture bridge break/collapse; lower counterweight activation; western hidden crew/holdout room; eastern arrival capture stage. Voluntary unfinished excavation pockets remain useful for future modular procedural recipes and human detail passes.
