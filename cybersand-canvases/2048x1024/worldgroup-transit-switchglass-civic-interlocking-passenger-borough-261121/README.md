## Structural thesis
This is an inhabited, *urban* transport environment, not another braking wheel/racing field. Four unequal industrial-civic stations form an asymmetrical horizontal borough: **West Passenger Terminal**, **Market Arcade**, **Municipal Signal Borough** and **East Maintenance/Departure Depot**. The subgrade has four physically entered archive, freight, cable relay and machinery bays. Large open sky and spare rooms are deliberate human-editor/procedural-authoring affordances.
