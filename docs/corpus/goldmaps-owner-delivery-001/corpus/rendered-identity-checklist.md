## What to check

- Match actual session map_id, Setup SHA-256, plane SHA-256, engine_head and source/derived_source to this lookup and the delivered roster; filenames alone are insufficient.
- Require a recorded non-headless renderer/display and verify each received PNG SHA-256 before visual inspection. Record actual executed product source separately from native qualification authority.
- Inspect the visible native map against the full-resolution source reference: landscape versus portrait, side elevation, frame extent, major voids/supports and no rotation/mirroring. A thumbnail or screenshot alone cannot prove every cell; the exact native material readback does that separately.
- The graphical session requests the accepted Workbench overview camera: set_camera(2) selects world origin (0,0) and the full authored Setup extent. Record clipping or stretching observed in the actual render; do not infer a visual PASS from this source behavior.
- Preserve original, Astra candidate, prior reference and policy-only descendant identity. Both policy descendants share their parent material plane and actor coordinates but have separate Setup bytes; original refusals remain visible and #183 remains outside Gold25.
- For archived comparison crops, translate the preserved randomized A/B/C labels with the exact card mapping below. Coordinates use x right/y down; xywh far edges are exclusive; named windows are observation bounds, not authorized edit masks.
- Initial *-gallery.png captures in the predecessor occur before QA_MAP selection and show the initial Gold25 selection recorded in the gallery receipt. Do not infer visible B3/D1/D2/descendant selection from a filename prefix.
- A normal screenshot does not establish physical NVIDIA rendering, route playfeel, synchronized Worlds, a new Art verdict or owner adoption. Preserve B3 inconclusive flight-speed judgement, D1 disagreement and D2 historical prior-reference preference.
