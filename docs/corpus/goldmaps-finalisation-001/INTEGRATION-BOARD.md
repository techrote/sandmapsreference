## Independence

All initial specialists inherit the mission's authorship mappings. Their new
architectural review is post-lock and explicitly unblinded. The randomized
comparison package has a separately committed answer key so a fresh assessor
can receive neutral material only. Model-role independence does not imply
independent human assessors or different model families.

Implementation authors cannot independently accept their own implementation.
Final cross-reviews must identify exact source HEAD or a source-content digest
with an explicit final-HEAD bridge. Evidence collected before subsequent code
changes is not silently promoted to those changed versions.

## Authority and evidence levels

Source-static, native hosted headless, graphical inspection, physical owner
NVIDIA playtest, artistic recommendation and owner acceptance are separate
fields. Unknown is not failure. A failed intervention is useful negative
evidence and is not automatically a bad source map.

## Productive continuation

When custody finishes, A audits source/consumer integrity and release safety.
When the native matrix stabilises, B investigates route limitations and kit
reliability. When C finishes the initial corpus review, its capacity is used
for a fresh blinded assessor and final scientific/architectural review.
The lead integrates evidence, fixes independently confirmed defects and
publishes the final decision packet on this one Draft/HOLD branch.
